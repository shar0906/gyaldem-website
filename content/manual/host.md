# Host guide: the host console

On show night, log in at **gyaldemsocialclub.com/admin** with your host login. You go straight to the host console for tonight's show. A host login that belongs to one series (like Please! A Ladies Night) only ever sees that series' shows. If two shows are on the same night, pick yours from the list at the top.

The console has three tabs: **Live**, **Bingo**, and **Guests**.

## Live

### Going live

Tap **We're live** when the night starts. Every guest who has the series page open switches to the live view on its own, and anyone who scans the table QR lands there too. Before that, guests see a countdown, then "Starting soon".

At the end of the night tap **Wrap the night**. The live page turns into a recap with the winners, a countdown to the next date, and the photo gallery link if there is one. Tapped it too early? **Reopen** puts the page back live.

### Updates

Type a short update (up to 280 characters) and tap **Post**. It goes to the top of **Updates** on the live page within a few seconds. **Remove** takes one down. Winners are posted here automatically when you announce them.

### Running order

The night's plan, shown on the live page under **Tonight**. Tap the circle next to an item to mark it as **on now**; it's highlighted for guests. Tap it again to clear. You can edit the items and times, add, or remove, then **Save**.

## Bingo

Three rounds a night, any line wins.

### Calling a line

1. Tap **Draw next**. Only your phone sees the line.
2. **Read it out.** On a Callout → Answer deck, the answer (like "Let's Stay Together by Al Green") is shown under the callout for you.
3. Tap **Called**. A few seconds later (5 by default) it appears on guests' phones.
4. Repeat. No line repeats within a round.

**Undo** takes back the line you just drew, or one you called that guests can't see yet. Once it shows **Live on guests' phones**, it stays.

On guests' phones, the answer to a callout appears once the next line is called, so the room has to listen.

**Called so far, A–Z** lists every line called this round, alphabetized for checking a card. The newest is in gold.

### When someone calls bingo

Check their card against **Called so far**. If it's good, tap **Someone has BINGO!**, type the winner's first name, and tap **Announce winner**. Confetti on your phone, and "Round 1 goes to Tasha!" on the live page. Then tap **Start round 2** when you're ready.

### More than one host phone

Only one phone calls at a time. The first phone to draw becomes the caller; any other phone follows along and shows who's calling. To switch, tap **Take over** on the phone that should call next.

## Guests

Everyone RSVP'd to tonight's show, with search. **Download** saves the list as a spreadsheet.

## If something looks off

- **Reconnecting…** means the signal dropped. Buttons pause until it's back, so nothing gets called twice.
- **No Bingo tab:** the show has no deck. Ask an admin to pick one in Ladies Night → Shows → Bingo deck.
- **Every line has been called:** the deck ran out for this round. Announce a winner or move to the next round.
