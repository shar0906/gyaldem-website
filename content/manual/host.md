# Host guide: bingo

On show night, log in at **gyaldemsocialclub.com/admin** with your host login. You go straight to the bingo caller. Before show night, the screen says there's no show tonight.

## Calling a round

1. Tap **Draw next line**.
2. **Read the line** out loud. The **answer on the card** (the artist) and the song are shown under it for you.
3. Guests mark that artist on their cards.
4. Repeat. No line repeats until you reset.

- **Undo last draw:** for a mis-tap. It takes back the most recent line.
- **Called so far, A–Z:** every artist called this round, alphabetized for checking a winner's card. The newest is highlighted in gold.

## When someone calls bingo

Check their card against **Called so far**. If it's good, tap **Bingo!** to end the round, with confetti on every host phone. Then tap **Reset for round 2** to start fresh.

## More than one host phone

Only one phone calls at a time. The first phone to draw becomes the caller. Any other host phone follows along, with a gold bar showing who's calling. To switch, tap **Take over** on the phone that should call next.

## If something looks off

- **Reconnecting…** means the signal dropped. Drawing pauses until it's back, so you can't draw the same line twice.
- **The deck is empty:** ask an admin to add lines in Ladies Night, Bingo deck.
- **Every line has been called:** the deck ran out for this round. Reset to start a new round.
