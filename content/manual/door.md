# Door guide

On show night, log in at **gyaldemsocialclub.com/admin** with your door login. You go straight to check-in. Before show night, the screen says there's no show tonight.

## Checking guests in

- The top shows **Checked in** and **VIP passes in**, each out of the total.
- **Search** by first name, last name, or email.
- Tap **Check in**. It turns green with the time. Tapped the wrong person? Tap it again to undo.
- Badges next to names:
  - **VIP × 2:** give that many VIP wristbands.
  - **Table for 4:** they booked a table through OpenTable for that many people.
  - **Walk-in:** they RSVP'd at the door.

Every door phone stays in sync: someone checked in on one phone shows as checked in on the others within a couple of seconds.

If the signal drops, a **Reconnecting…** bar appears and the list shows the last update. It catches up on its own.

## Walk-ins

Tap **Add walk-in** at the bottom.

1. **Guest has a phone:** they scan the QR code with their camera and RSVP on their own phone, which checks them in. If their phone already knows them from an earlier RSVP, they're checked in the moment the page opens.
2. **No phone:** type their first name, last name, and email. Check **Guest agreed to RSVP and join the Gyal Dem mailing list** only after asking them. Tap **RSVP and check in**.

The QR code only works on show night.

## Can't find someone

- Try their last name or email instead.
- Still nothing? Add them as a walk-in.
- An RSVP doesn't guarantee a table; that's handled by the restaurant through OpenTable.
