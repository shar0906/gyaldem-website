# Artist guide

Welcome to Ladies Night. You log in at **gyaldemsocialclub.com/admin** with the email you were invited with. Your dashboard has three tabs: **Propose**, **Profile**, and **Results**.

Forgot your password? Ask Gyal Dem to resend your invite; it arrives as a password-reset email.

## Profile

This is what guests see on the ballot page when you're performing. Nothing you change here is public until Gyal Dem approves it.

- **Profile photo:** square works best. JPG, PNG, or WebP, up to 5 MB.
- **Cover photo:** fills the sign-in page behind the form. Portrait, at least 1080 × 1920, up to 10 MB. Keep your face above the dashed line in the preview; the form covers the rest.
- **Name guests see**, **Instagram**, and **Website** (optional). Your logo in the page footer links to your website, or your Instagram if you don't add one.
- **Logo** (optional): PNG or WebP with a transparent background, up to 2 MB. It sits on a dark background, so light versions look best.
- **Bio:** up to 280 characters.
- **Ballot colors:** a primary color (background and cards) and an accent (buttons and picks). If text on your colors would be hard to read, you'll see a red note and can't submit until you adjust them.

The **live preview** shows your sign-in page and ballot as you edit.

Click **Submit for review**. The banner at the top tells you where things stand:

| Banner | Means |
|---|---|
| **Pending review** | Gyal Dem has your changes. Guests still see your current profile. |
| **Changes requested** | Gyal Dem sent it back, with their note. Update and submit again. |
| **Live** | Approved. This is what guests see. |
| **Not public yet** | Fill it in and submit to get started. |

You can edit again while it's pending; your latest version replaces the earlier one.

## Propose

Build the list guests vote on.

- **Your library** (left): every song you've ever added, ready to reuse.
- **Search Apple Music** (middle): find songs and tap **Add**. Can't find it? **Add it by hand** with the title and original artist.
- **Your list** (right): the songs for your next show. Use the arrows to reorder. Guests see them in this order, so put the ones you most want to sing first.

When you're booked on a show, you need **at least 10 songs**. Tap **Submit list** at the bottom. You can come back, edit, and submit again until Gyal Dem publishes the ballot.

Once the ballot is published, the list is locked because guests are voting on it. To change something after that, ask Gyal Dem.

**Not booked yet?** The same tools save songs to your library, so you're ready when you are.

## Results

Each of your shows appears here. Results unlock when voting closes, and you'll also get them by email.

- **Your songs ranked** by votes, with the share of guests who picked each one.
- **Guests who shared their info with you:** only guests who checked the box to share. **Export CSV** downloads their names and emails.
