# What guests see

Each series has its own link to share: **gyaldemsocialclub.com/ladies-night/an-ode-to-her** and **gyaldemsocialclub.com/ladies-night/please**. **/ladies-night** is the hub that lists every series with its next date. Pages are built for phones and show the series' next upcoming show in its logos and colors. Knowing this flow helps when a guest asks for help.

Which steps a guest sees depends on what the series uses (set in Series). Voting only appears on People's Choice series; the Posh step only on series that use Posh.

## Before RSVP opens

A countdown to the RSVP opening time, with no form. When the countdown ends, the page switches to the sign-in form on its own.

## Signing in (the gate)

The series' cover photo fills the screen, with its logos at the top, then the tagline, date, time, venue, and "Music by …" when there's a DJ. The series, venue, and Gyal Dem logos sit at the bottom. It fits on one screen.

- Guests enter **first name, last name, and email**, then tap **Let me in**. The line under the button tells them this RSVPs them, adds them to the Gyal Dem mailing list, and (for a client's series) shares their RSVP with the client.
- On People's Choice series, **Share my name and email with [artist]** is optional and unchecked. Only guests who check it appear on the artist's results page.
- **Already RSVP'd?** asks only for the email. If it's not on the list for this show, the full form opens.

After that, the guest's phone **remembers them**. Coming back later opens "Welcome back, [name]." with everything they've done. **Not you?** forgets that phone, for shared devices.

## People's Choice ballot

Once voting is open and the ballot is published:

- The artist's photo, name, Instagram, and bio at the top.
- One list of songs. Guests tap hearts to pick **up to 5**, then **Submit**.
- They can **Change my picks** until voting closes. After that, their picks are locked and shown on their summary.

## Posh

On series that use Posh, the summary leads with **Confirm your spot on Posh**: it opens the client's Posh page in a new tab, where they RSVP there too (Posh requires its own login). When they come back, **I'm on Posh** clears the step.

## VIP

When VIP is on, sales haven't closed, and passes are left, guests see the offer right after RSVPing or voting: price, perks, and how many passes they want (up to 5). Payment happens on Stripe's secure page, then they come back to the summary with "You're VIP." Stripe emails the receipt.

## The summary

Where every guest ends up:

- **Chips** for what they've done: RSVP'd, Voted, Table for 4 · 7:30 PM, VIP × 2.
- **Save your seat:** an RSVP doesn't hold a table. Guests pick **how many in your party**, tap **Find my table**, and OpenTable opens over the page, already set to the show's date, start time, and party size. When they finish booking, it closes on its own and their table shows as booked. **I've reserved my table** is there for anyone who books another way.
- **Your picks**, and whether voting is still open.
- **When / Where**, plus **Artist**, **Music**, and **Theme** when set. Tapping the venue opens Google Maps.
- **Countdown** to the show, then **Starting soon** on the night.
- **Make it VIP**, if passes are still available.
- **Add to calendar.** The invite has the venue's full address and the live page link.
- The **footer** shows the client (or artist), venue, and Gyal Dem logos, each linking to their site.

## The live page

When the host taps **We're live**, every guest with the series page open switches to the live view: the current bingo line, **Called so far**, **Updates** from the host (winners included), and the running order with what's on now. The table placards' QR codes open the same page at `/ladies-night/<series>/live`, which anyone can open without RSVPing.

After the host wraps the night, the live page shows a recap: the winners, a countdown to the next date, and the photo gallery link when there is one.

## Show night at the door

The door shows a QR code. A guest who scans it lands on the series page and is **checked in**: instantly if their phone already knows them, or after entering their details if not. They see "You're checked in. Enjoy the night."

## Emails guests get

- **Stripe receipt**, if they buy VIP.
- **OpenTable confirmation**, if they book a table.
- **The reminder**, at 6 PM the evening before (unless changed), from Kit, with the live page link.
- Future Gyal Dem emails, since RSVPing adds them to the mailing list.

## Sharing the link

Shared in iMessage, Instagram, WhatsApp, or X, the link shows a preview card with the series' photo (or artist's), the show, the date, and the venue. It updates on its own within about 10 minutes of changes.
