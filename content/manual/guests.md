# What guests see

Guests go to **gyaldemsocialclub.com/ladies-night**. It's built for phones and shows the next upcoming show in the artist's colors. Knowing this flow helps when a guest asks for help.

## Before RSVP opens

A countdown to the RSVP opening time, with no form. When the countdown ends, the page switches to the sign-in form on its own.

## Signing in (the gate)

The artist's cover photo fills the screen, with the show's headline and description.

- Guests enter **first name, last name, and email**, then tap **Let me in**. The line under the button tells them this RSVPs them and adds them to the Gyal Dem mailing list.
- **Share my name and email with [artist]** is optional and unchecked. Only guests who check it appear on the artist's results page.
- **Already RSVP'd?** asks only for the email. If it's not on the list for this show, the full form opens.

After that, the guest's phone **remembers them**. Coming back later opens "Welcome back, [name]." with everything they've done. **Not you?** forgets that phone, for shared devices.

## People's Choice ballot

Once voting is open and the ballot is published:

- The artist's photo, name, Instagram, and bio at the top.
- One list of songs. Guests tap hearts to pick **up to 5**, then **Submit**.
- They can **Change my picks** until voting closes. After that, their picks are locked and shown on their summary.

## VIP

When VIP is on and passes are left, guests see the offer right after RSVPing or voting: price, perks, and how many passes they want (up to 5). Payment happens on Stripe's secure page, then they come back to the summary with "You're VIP." Stripe emails the receipt.

## The summary

Where every guest ends up:

- **Chips** for what they've done: RSVP'd, Voted, Table for 4 · 7:30 PM, VIP × 2.
- **Save your seat:** an RSVP doesn't hold a table. Guests pick **how many in your party**, tap **Find my table**, and OpenTable opens over the page, already set to the show's date, start time, and party size. When they finish booking, it closes on its own and their table shows as booked. **I've reserved my table** is there for anyone who books another way.
- **Your picks**, and whether voting is still open.
- **When / Where / Artist.** Tapping the venue opens Google Maps.
- **Make it VIP**, if passes are still available.
- **Add to calendar.**
- The **footer** shows the venue, artist, and Gyal Dem logos, each linking to their site.

## Show night at the door

The door shows a QR code. A guest who scans it lands on `/ladies-night` and is **checked in**: instantly if their phone already knows them, or after entering their details if not. They see "You're checked in. Enjoy the night."

## Emails guests get

- **Stripe receipt**, if they buy VIP.
- **OpenTable confirmation**, if they book a table.
- **The reminder**, at 6 PM the evening before (unless changed), from Kit.
- Future Gyal Dem emails, since RSVPing adds them to the mailing list.

## Sharing the link

Shared in iMessage, Instagram, WhatsApp, or X, the link shows a preview card with the artist's photo, the show, the date, and the venue. It updates on its own within about 10 minutes of changes.
